# Three-minute demo script

1. Start with `DEMO_MODE=recorded-live`, open Opportunities, and identify the banner as a timestamped replay of completed live runs across Australia, Canada, and the United States.
2. Open Evidence to trace a normalized ASL deadline back to `closing_date_raw` and the official portal.
3. Open Raw and show the artifact-level extraction notice.
4. In Discover, show 1,000+ open opportunities, expand the advanced filters, and click **Find more opportunities**—there is no operator login. Then open Operations and show the completed CanadaBuys, NYC, Montgomery County, and San Francisco runs.
5. Show the self-healing ledger: AAI's same-ID approved repair and CCA's contained adapter fallback after Bright retained a nested wrapper.
6. Open Workspace, complete a task, then open Copilot and ask what must be verified. Show the draft boundary and exact evidence chips on the response.
7. Close with the source-adapter architecture: new jurisdictions are data, not UI branches.

Only call an artifact “live” when the proof ledger identifies it as a completed dataset run. Preview replay, creation envelopes, and healing proposals must keep their exact labels.
